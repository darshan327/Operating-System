   COOLING SYSTEM

     What is the Cooling System?

       The cooling system removes heat produced by:
       
       CPU
       GPU
       Power components
       
       It keeps the laptop at a safe temperature.

       Main Parts of Cooling System
           1. Cooling Fans
           
           The two circular fans in your laptop.
           
           Function
           Pull cool air inside
           Push hot air outside
           Real-life example
           
           While gaming or coding:
           
           CPU temperature increases
           Fans spin faster to cool system

       2. Heat Pipes

           The black pipes connecting components to fans.
           
           Function
           Carry heat away from CPU/GPU
           Transfer heat to cooling fins
           Real-life example
           
           Like water pipes carrying hot water away from a machine.  

       3. Heat Sink Fins

           Thin metal fins near the exhaust area.
           
           Function
           Release heat into air
           
           Fans blow air through these fins to remove heat.   



       Purpose of Cooling System

           Prevent Overheating
           
           Without cooling:
           
           CPU becomes extremely hot
           Laptop may shut down
           Performance drops

           Maintain Performance
           
               Cooling helps CPU run at full speed.
               
               Example
               
               During:
               
               Gaming
               Video editing
               Programming
               
               cooling prevents lag and slowdown.
           
        Protect Hardware
           
             Too much heat can damage:
             
             CPU
             GPU
             Battery
             Motherboard
             
           Cooling increases laptop lifespan.
           
        Real-Life Scenario
           Example 1 – Gaming
           
           While playing GTA or Valorant:
           
           CPU/GPU work heavily
           Temperature rises
           Fans spin quickly
           Hot air comes out from side vents
