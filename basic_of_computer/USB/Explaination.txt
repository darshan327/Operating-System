    UNIVERSAL SERIAL BUS

      It is used to connect external devices to a computer.

      Purpose of USB Port

         USB ports help the laptop:
         
         Transfer data
         Connect devices
         Supply power/charging

        Functions of USB
          1. Data Transfer
          
          Used to copy:
          
          Photos
          Videos
          Documents
          Software
          
          between devices. 

          2. Connect Input Devices

             USB connects:
             
             Keyboard
             Mouse
             Game controller
             Printer

         3. Charging Devices

             USB can provide electrical power.
             
             Real-life example
             
             You charge:
             
             Mobile phone
             Smartwatch
             Earbuds
             
             through laptop USB port. 

         4. Connect Storage Devices

           USB connects:
           
           Pen drives
           External hard disks
           SSDs
           Real-life example
           
           You store backup of:
           
           Photos
           Projects
           Movies
           
           on external drive.    

        5. Multimedia Devices

             USB supports:
             
             Webcam
             Microphone
             Speakers
             Real-life example
             
             During online classes:
             
             Webcam and mic connect through USB.
